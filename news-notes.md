## 8. BepiColombo arrives at Mercury after eight years
- The joint ESA–JAXA BepiColombo mission arrived at Mercury after an
  eight-year, 6-billion-mile journey; flight controllers fire its
  thrusters on Saturday 21 Nov to enter orbit, making it only the
  second mission ever to circle Mercury. Science operations begin
  April 2027.
- Sources: The Guardian via skepticsociety.co.uk 20 Nov 2026;
  National Geographic 20 Nov 2026; Scientific American 20 Nov 2026;
  ESA Operations LinkedIn post 20 Nov 2026; skyandtelescope.org
  20 Nov 2026.

## 9. Jury convicts man who started the Palisades Fire
